Welcome to Cybersecurity Awareness

Protect your digital life with knowledge and best practices.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.

Common Threats

Phishing, malware, ransomware, and social engineering are some of the most common online threats.

Best Practices

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, and avoid suspicious links to stay secure.

Network Security

Secure your Wi-Fi, use VPNs, and monitor your network for any unusual activities.
