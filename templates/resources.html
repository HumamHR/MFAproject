Cybersecurity Roadmaps and Resources

Explore comprehensive guides and recommended resources for both Red Team and Blue Team professionals.

Red Team Roadmap

Follow these steps to become a Red Team professional, focusing on offensive security and ethical hacking.

  • Step 1: Learn the basics of networking, Linux, and Windows systems administration.
  • Step 2: Study ethical hacking concepts and methodologies with certifications like CEH or OSCP.
  • Step 3: Practice penetration testing on platforms like Hack The Box and TryHackMe.
  • Step 4: Master offensive tools such as Metasploit, Burp Suite, and Nmap.
  • Step 5: Advance to exploit development and vulnerability research using resources like Exploit Education.

Blue Team Roadmap

Develop defensive security skills to protect networks, detect threats, and respond to incidents effectively.

  • Step 1: Build a strong foundation in networking and operating system fundamentals.
  • Step 2: Learn about incident response and detection methods (GCIH).
  • Step 3: Gain expertise with SIEM tools like Splunk or ELK Stack.
  • Step 4: Study digital forensics and malware analysis with courses like SANS DFIR.
  • Step 5: Practice threat hunting and mitigation in real-world scenarios.
